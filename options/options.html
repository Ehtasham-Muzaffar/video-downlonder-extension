Platform Guide & How-To

Learn how to easily capture and download videos on each platform.

Facebook

Downloading Facebook Videos & Reels

  • Open any Facebook video, Reel, or Watch stream.
  • Press Play on the video so the browser starts buffering the stream.
  • Hover over the video to click the floating ⬇ Download button, or click the Nova icon in the Chrome toolbar.
  • The popup displays the highest available MP4 stream ready for instant 1-click download.
Instagram

Downloading Instagram Reels & Posts

  • Navigate to Instagram and open any post or Reel.
  • When the Reel starts playing, Nova's in-page download pill appears on the top-right corner.
  • Click the download pill or open the Nova popup to download the full HD MP4 file.
  • The post creator handle (e.g. @username) is automatically included in the filename!
YouTube Notice

Understanding YouTube Policy

  • Chrome Web Store policies strictly forbid extensions from providing 1-click downloads for YouTube videos due to Google's Terms of Service.
  • Nova Downloader detects YouTube videos, displays their title and channel metadata, and provides detected stream addresses or copy links.
  • For direct 1-click HD downloads, Nova works without restrictions on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Twitter/X, Reddit, Vimeo, and thousands of video sites!
Universal Web

Any Video Site / Web Page

  • Nova features a dual-layer media scanner: Network Sniffer (captures live MP4, WEBM, M3U8 streams) + DOM Scanner (detects HTML5 video players).
  • Works on news websites, blogs, streaming portals, and social networks automatically.
